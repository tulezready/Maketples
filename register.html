Registration

Sell your products beyond your district.

If you run a business in East New Britain, you can apply to sell on MaketPles ENB. The Division of Commerce & Industry reviews every application, so buyers know each business listed is a real, verified local enterprise.

01

Apply

Fill in this form. It takes about five minutes and you only need your business details and a phone number.

02

We review

The Division checks your application. If something is missing, we contact you rather than reject it.

03

Start listing

Once approved you get your own login to add products, set prices and receive orders.
